## Ranking Method 3: Monetization Velocity
*Ranked by how quickly the output can realistically be converted into revenue or savings.*

1. **Task Queue Optimizer (05):** Immediate impact; lowering compute costs instantly saves money on every execution.
2. **Lead Dataset Generator (01/02):** High-quality, niche data lists can be sold instantly to B2B buyers.
3. **Bug Bounty Patcher (03):** Security patches have clear, relatively fast bounty payouts once merged.
4. **Affiliate Guide (10):** Can generate passive revenue quickly if targeting low-competition keywords.
5. **Micro-SaaS Prototyper (07/08):** Requires a product launch, but simple tools can be monetized in weeks.
6. **Newsletter Agent (04):** Takes time to build a readership before programmatic ads or sponsorships pay off.
7. **SEO Article Writer (06):** Slow organic traffic growth; takes months to rank in search engines.
8. **Data Feed Architect (09):** Requires building and selling a complex B2B subscription model.
9. **Agency Outreach (15):** Direct sales to state agencies, but B2G sales cycles are notoriously slow.
10. **Budget Analyzer (12):** Early-stage B2G research that kicks off a long sales cycle.
11. **RFP Monitor (13):** Requires writing formal proposals and waiting for government awards.
12. **Grant Researcher (11):** Government grant applications have very long approval windows.
13. **Funding Strategist (14):** Internal business planning; generates strategy, not direct revenue.
